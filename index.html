Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kinbit ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect several types of information from and about users of our App, including:

  • Email Address: Collected when you sign in to use the Tribe feature, via our authentication provider (Clerk).
  • Display Name: A name you choose when creating or joining a tribe. This is visible to other members of your tribe.
  • Habit Data: Habit titles, descriptions, log history, and streak counts. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Tribe Activity: Habit check-ins, streak progress, and activity feed entries that are synced to our backend and shared with your tribe members.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the App, including features used and time spent.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and technical logs used for crash reporting and stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality.
  • Manage your account and provide customer support.
  • Share your habit progress, streaks, and activity with members of your tribe on the leaderboard and activity feed.
  • Improve and optimize the App experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Shared With Other Users

If you join or create a tribe, your display name, current streak, and habit check-in activity are visible to other members of that tribe. This information is stored on our servers (Convex) and synced in real time. No other personal information (such as your email address) is shared with other users.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Clerk — Authentication provider (sign-in, account management)
  • Convex — Database and backend hosting for tribe data
  • Expo — Push notifications and crash reporting

Please review the privacy policies of these providers for more details on their data practices.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide you with our services and for legitimate legal purposes.

Account Deletion: You may request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at shoukuvallil@gmail.com. Upon request, we will delete your personal data from our active databases within 30 days, unless required otherwise by law. Habit data stored locally on your device can be removed by uninstalling the App.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
