Delete Your Account

Kinbit — developed by VZOUQ

You can request deletion of your Kinbit account and associated data at any time. Follow the steps below and we will process your request within 30 days.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to shoukuvallil@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Kinbit Account
  3. Include the email address associated with your Kinbit account
  4. We will confirm receipt and process the deletion within 30 days
You do not need to be currently signed in to submit a deletion request. Simply email us from any address and include your Kinbit account email in the message.

What Gets Deleted

Data Where stored On deletion
Email address & authentication data Clerk (auth provider) Deleted — account removed from Clerk within 30 days
Display name Convex (backend) Deleted — removed from all tribe memberships
Tribe memberships & streak data Convex (backend) Deleted — your entries removed from all tribes you belong to
Activity feed entries Convex (backend) Deleted — your past check-ins removed from tribe activity feeds
Habit data, logs & reminders Local device storage Not deleted remotely — stored only on your device. To remove, uninstall the Kinbit app.

Retention Period

All server-side data is deleted within 30 days of a confirmed request, unless retention is required by law. Local data stored on your device is not accessible to us and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Contact

For questions about data deletion or this policy, email us at shoukuvallil@gmail.com.
